The Bounce Formula

Five things. One bigger vert.

A big jump takes more than strong legs. Bounce Method trains all five qualities that decide how high you go.

01

Strength

Build the force you push into the floor.

02

Power

Turn that strength into fast, explosive jumps.

03

Elasticity

Train your tendons to spring like a pogo stick.

04

Speed

Get quicker into your takeoff.

05

Jump mechanics

Clean approach, arm swing and takeoff.

The key: train all five together.

Your week

Every day has a purpose.

A simple weekly plan you can repeat, built around your practices and games.

  1. Mon

    Force Day

    Lower body strength.

    High intensity
  2. Tue

    Tendon, Ankle + Balance

    Stay springy and durable.

    Lower intensity
  3. Wed

    Elasticity + Speed

    Quick, reactive jumps and acceleration.

    High intensity
  4. Thu

    Rest

    Recover and grow.

    Recovery
  5. Fri

    Power + Max Jump

    Go all out and test your vert.

    High intensity
  6. Sat

    Durability Reset

    Light mobility and stability work.

    Lower intensity
  7. Sun

    Rest

    Get ready for the next week.

    Recovery

Playing a lot that week? Dial down the volume and keep the quality high.
